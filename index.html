What is a Monthly Budget Tracker?

A monthly budget tracker is a simple tool to plan where your money goes before the month begins. Instead of wondering where your salary went, you allocate every rupee/dollar to needs, wants, savings, and bills.

Our free budget tracker app helps you create a zero-based budget, track daily spending, and see exactly how much you have left to spend. Unlike complex Excel sheets, it automatically calculates your total income, total expenses, and remaining balance.

Key Features

  • Zero-Based Budgeting: Assign every dollar of income to a category until income − expenses = 0.

  • Export & Save: Download your monthly budget as CSV or save as PDF. Load last month's data in one click.

How to Use This Budget Tracker in 3 Steps

  2. Add Expense Categories: Add rent, groceries, bills, transport, etc. and set a budget for each.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this monthly budget tracker really free?

Yes, 100% free forever. No premium plans, no sign-up, no hidden fees. We built it to be a simple alternative to paid apps like YNAB and Mint.

How do I track my monthly budget effectively?

Use the 50/30/20 rule. Allocate 50% of your income to needs, 30% to wants, and 20% to savings. Our tracker lets you create these categories and see if you are over budget in real-time.

Is my financial data safe?

Yes. This is a browser-based tracker. All your data is saved in your localStorage, not on our servers. No one can see your budget, not even us.

Can I use this for zero-based budgeting?

Yes. This tool is built for zero-based budgeting. Your goal is to make Income − Expenses − Savings = 0, so every dollar has a job.

What is the difference between a budget planner and an expense tracker?

A budget planner plans money before you spend, an expense tracker records what you already spent. Our app does both — you plan your budget at the start of the month and track expenses daily.

Can I save my budget for next month?

Yes, click "Duplicate for Next Month" and your categories will be copied. You can also export your data to CSV.

Can I export my budget data?

Yes. Download your monthly budget as a CSV file with one click. Every category, the total expenses, the income and the remaining balance are included.

Does this budget tracker work offline?

Yes. Once the page has loaded you can add expenses and edit your budget without internet. Your data is stored locally in your browser.
